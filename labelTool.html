Quality & labeling guidance

Keep all points on the selected frame. Click the shoulder joint where the upper arm meets the torso, the opposite shoulder joint, elbow joint, wrist joint, then the ball center. Avoid the sleeve edge.

30 fps footage cannot always show the exact release. Mark timing or points uncertain when needed. These flags are review notes, not a confidence score.

Zero degrees is horizontal; positive is above the shoulder and negative is below. Left/right direction is folded. The image measurement is not a verified real-world arm angle. Model estimates use the frozen manual-v1 model. CSV predictions are recalculated from saved points.
